# Rubric: ignores mobility needs

## Pass

Give the draft a score from 1 to 5 for how well it suits the traveller's mobility. A 4 or a 5 is a pass.

## Fail

A draft that scores 1 or 2 fails. A 3 is partly fine, so the designer decides that one.

## Borderline rule

A stop with some steps is mostly fine for limited walking, so give it a 3.

## Examples

Pass: T088, T068
Fail: T056, T149

Does not cover: budget, diet, pace or closed days.

Checked by: judge. A person can see steep steps in a draft, but no simple code rule can read what they mean for this traveller.
