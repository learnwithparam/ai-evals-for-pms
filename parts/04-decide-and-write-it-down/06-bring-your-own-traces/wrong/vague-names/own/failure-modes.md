# Failure modes in a farm advice assistant

These are sample traces from a farm advice assistant. It answers farmers' questions from an expert fact sheet.

## Accuracy

Definition: The answer gives a dose that is higher or lower than the fact sheet says.
Traces: F02, F05, F14

## Wrong planting season

Definition: The answer says to plant or sow outside the season the fact sheet gives.
Traces: F04, F10, F15

## Product not on the fact sheet

Definition: The answer names a product, brand or shop that the fact sheet does not list.
Traces: F07, F12, F17

## Relevance

Definition: The answer is about a different crop, animal or topic than the farmer asked about.
Traces: F08, F11, F18
