# A/B test decision: instant drafts for city breaks

Designer booking rate: 18.0% (216 of 1,200 sessions)
Instant booking rate: 20.0% (240 of 1,200 sessions)
Difference: 2.0 points
95% interval: -1.1 to 5.1 points
Crosses zero: yes
Refund guardrail: breached. Closed on arrival refunds are 1.17% of instant sessions (14 of 1,200), against 0.17% with a designer. The limit is 0.5%.
Decision: ship to all

## Why

The lift could be zero, because the interval crosses zero. And more customers ask for refunds because a place was closed when they arrived. Instant drafts skip the designer, who used to catch closed days.

Split by trip type, the lift comes from Lisbon weekends: 92 bookings of 400 with instant drafts, against 68 with a designer. Next, we fix closed days in the draft, then test instant drafts again on Lisbon weekends only.
