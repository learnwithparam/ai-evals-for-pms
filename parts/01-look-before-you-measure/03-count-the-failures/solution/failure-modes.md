# Failure modes

## Ignores mobility needs
Definition: The draft books a place with steps for a traveller who uses a wheelchair or cannot walk far.
Traces: T020, T199, T207, T255

## Closed on the day
Definition: The draft books a place on a day the catalogue says it is closed.
Traces: T122, T157, T221

## Over budget
Definition: The total cost a person is more than the budget on the request.
Traces: T187, T267

## Impossible travel
Definition: Two stops in a row are too far apart to reach in the time between them.
Traces: T115, T258

## Wrong food for the diet
Definition: A meal or tasting does not fit the diet the customer asked for.
Traces: T103, T119
